### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Computer Engineer** combining deep full-stack engineering expertise with an entrepreneurial mindset to craft scalable, AI-driven digital ecosystems. 

Over the course of my career, I've had the privilege of engineering enterprise-grade solutions at **Microsoft** (leveraging the .NET ecosystem, Azure AI, and microservice architectures) and driving high-throughput fintech projects at **ParamTech**. Concurrently, as the **Co-Founder of Force Studio**, I lead end-to-end digital transformation, venture development, and AI automation for global clients.

- 🤖 **AI & Autonomous Agents**: Building multi-agent systems, LLM integrations, and intelligent workflow automations using n8n and modern frameworks.
- ⚡ **Full-Stack & Cloud Architecture**: Designing high-performance distributed backends (.NET, Node.js, Azure) coupled with reactive web & mobile frontends.
- 🎨 **Creative Web & 3D Experiences**: Crafting immersive interactive user experiences with Three.js, Babylon.js, and Electron.
- 🌍 **Academic Foundation**: B.S. in Computer Engineering *(Bilecik Şeyh Edebali University)* & B.S. in Management Information Systems *(Ankara University)*.
